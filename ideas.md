## Selected Design: Option 1 — Elevated Scandinavian Minimalism meets California Warmth

**Rationale:** The ICP (overloaded professional mom) responds to calm, premium aesthetics. She shops at Whole Foods, drives a BMW X3, and pays for Lululemon. Her visual language is warm neutrals, editorial whitespace, and understated luxury — not bold navy or terracotta. The forest green accent signals trust and nature (clean, organic, safe) without the corporate feel of navy. The asymmetric editorial layout with an embedded hero form is the highest-converting structure for paid traffic landing pages — form above the fold, no scroll required to convert.

**Key design decisions:**
- Font: Playfair Display (headlines) + DM Sans (body) — loaded via Google Fonts
- Colors: Cream background, forest green accent, warm sand secondary
- Layout: Left-heavy hero with inline form, editorial section breaks
- No purple gradients, no Inter-only typography, no centered grid monotony
